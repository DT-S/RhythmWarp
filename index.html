RECORDING INFO

Auto log records each Start → Stop session, using your exercise and category at Start. Count-in is excluded. Silent training bars count. Stop ends the entry; restart creates another. Disabling auto log and changing its minimum duration apply to the next session. Sessions shorter than your chosen threshold are discarded and never stored.

DISPLAY INFO

Choose 70–110%. iPhone uses a dedicated compact layout. Saved on this device and included in backups; loading a rhythm preset keeps your display size. Browser-level pinch and double-tap zoom are disabled; use Interface Size to resize the WARP.

Interface feedback

HAPTICS INFO

Haptics use device vibration where supported. iPhone Safari may not provide vibration. The RHYTHM WARP start screen is on by default; disable it to open the metronome directly. Starting through START RHYTHM WARP allows a reliable user-triggered launch sound, unlike browser-blocked autoplay. iOS Reduce Motion disables motion by default; turn on OVERRIDE DEVICE REDUCED MOTION only if you deliberately want the full RHYTHM WARP effects.

CUSTOM SOUND MEMORY

SOUND IMPORT INFO

Up to 16 sounds, 2 MB and 5 seconds each. MP3, WAV and other formats your browser can decode. Stored locally and included in JSON backups. Select each sound in Audio Matrix for any channel and accent. Imported sounds play unchanged; volume follows the channel mixer. Stop playback before uploading or deleting.
